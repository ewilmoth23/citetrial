Northbridge Loop Pilot: First Six Months

The fictional City of Northbridge began the Harbor Loop transit pilot on April 3, 2032.

Ridership

Validated weekday boardings on the loop averaged 8,240 in September, compared with a baseline average of 6,800 during March. The comparison represents a 21 percent increase after rounding.

The pilot added six battery-electric buses and service every twelve minutes between 6 a.m. and 10 p.m.

Limits

Transit staff cautioned that construction near Central Station may have shifted riders onto the loop. The city had not completed a controlled analysis of the cause.

Boarding totals show use, but they do not by themselves explain why use changed.
