NORTHBRIDGE DEPARTMENT OF MOBILITY - SYNTHETIC PRESS RELEASE
April 3, 2032

The fictional Harbor Loop pilot begins service today with six battery-electric buses. Scheduled service runs every twelve minutes from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m.

The department expects the route to improve connections among Harbor Point, Central Station, and East Market. This expectation is a program goal, not a measured outcome.
