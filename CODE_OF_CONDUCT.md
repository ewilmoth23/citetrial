# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in CiteTrail a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Contributors should use welcoming language, respect different viewpoints, accept constructive feedback,
focus on the community's benefit, and show empathy. Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, sexualized
content, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for behavior they reasonably consider inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately to
the maintainers listed in repository ownership metadata. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and
confidentially. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

This document is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
